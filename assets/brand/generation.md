# Selected compass logo · Generation brief

- **Selected concept:** B · Open ring / 开放圆环, selected by the user from four review concepts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-17.
- **Generation mode:** built-in `image_gen` through the imagegen skill.
- **Published asset:** [`agentcaptain-mark.png`](agentcaptain-mark.png).
- **Reference:** a crop of the earlier application-header compass was supplied only as a style reference. The brief explicitly requested a new emblem, not a copy or tracing of its geometry.
- **Pixel treatment:** the selected B image is used unchanged; its pixels were not cropped, recolored, composited or otherwise edited for publication.

The deep ink background and generous clear space are part of the generated
image. The product name and tagline are native text outside the logo, not
raster lettering embedded in it. See [brand usage](README.md) for placement.

## Exact generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original AgentCaptain compass logo concept for user review, symbol only.
Input image: the supplied application-header crop is a STYLE REFERENCE only. Use its understated compass mood and mint/teal identity; ignore all text, header layout and exact old geometry. Design a NEW emblem, not a copy or tracing.
Style/medium: exceptionally simple elegant flat geometric logo, balanced negative space, confident clean edges, readable at small app-icon sizes. A compass must be the immediately recognizable idea.
Composition: ONE centered symbol on a square, uniform solid deep ink background approximately #101B21. Mark occupies roughly 60 percent of canvas width with generous clear space. All variants use this same presentation for fair comparison.
Color palette: pale mint #A7EAD1 and restrained teal #5BAEBE, opaque flat fills and strokes, high contrast.
Constraints: no wordmark, no letters, no A monogram, no numerals, no labels, no slogan. Original design only. No gradients, shading, grain, shadows, 3D, glow, badges, mockups, watermarks, tiny network dots, circuit motifs, robot faces, ornamental nautical details, fine decorative dial markings. Not a sheet of alternatives: one finished emblem only.
Design direction: An intentionally open circular arc with one clean gap at northeast. A single slender two-tone direction needle points through the gap, giving a sense of guidance and forward movement. No crosshair, no cardinal ticks, no dots, no extra rings. The open arc and needle must be optically balanced, not look like a loading spinner or power button.
```
